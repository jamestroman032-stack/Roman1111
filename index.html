Five-Tick Candlestick Strategy

Uses the money-management multiplier below after each loss.
Strategy: group incoming live ticks into five-tick candles. When a completed candle closes above its open (green), buy RISE for the next 5 ticks. When it closes below its open (red), buy FALL for the next 5 ticks. Flat candles are skipped. After every loss in single-win mode, the bot doubles the stake and alternates market families: a Volatility loss switches to any available Step Index (100–500), and a Step Index loss switches back to a Volatility market. It continues this alternating recovery until the first win, then stops. Continuous mode doubles after losses, resets stake after wins, changes to another market after each win, and stops at session take profit or another enabled risk limit.
