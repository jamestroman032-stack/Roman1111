Even / Odd — 1-Second Volatility Strategy

Strategy: scans only Volatility 10/15/25/30/50 (1s). It counts digits in the recent sample; each digit occurring above 10% contributes to its parity side. The side with more qualifying digits becomes the bias. To trade EVEN, wait for the configured consecutive ODD ticks; to trade ODD, wait for consecutive EVEN ticks. Continuous trading continues toward session take profit. These digit patterns do not guarantee an edge or profit.
